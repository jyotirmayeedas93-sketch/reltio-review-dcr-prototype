    How confidence signals work

    Signals help you decide where to look. They aren't a verdict.

    What each signal means

    High confidence
    The proposed value is consistent with reference data and with the other fields on this record.
    Needs review
    Something about the change can't be verified or carries risk. Read the reasoning before you decide.
    Not enough signal
    There's nothing to check this value against. It's your judgment alone, and the signal says so rather than guessing.

    What it does

    • Checks each proposed value on its own, against reference sources and the rest of the record.
    • Shows its reasoning on every field, so you can disagree with it.

    What it never does

    • Approve, reject or edit a change. Every decision is yours.
    • Hide a field. Every proposed change is always listed.

    In this prototype the signals and reasoning are demo data, not a live model.
